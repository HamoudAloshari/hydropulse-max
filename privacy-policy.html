Privacy Policy for HydroPulse Max

Last updated: November 2026

HydroPulse Max ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage.

1. Microphone & Audio Usage

Our application requests access to the device microphone strictly to measure real-time ambient sound levels (Decibel Meter). We do not record, store, transmit, or share any audio data. All sound analysis is performed locally and ephemerally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To keep this application 100% free, we utilize trusted advertising networks including Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Start.io. These third-party services may collect anonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic data to deliver personalized or contextual advertisements in compliance with applicable privacy regulations.

3. Offline Functionality

All core cleaning, frequency generation, and diagnostic tools function completely offline without transmitting personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

HydroPulse Max is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
