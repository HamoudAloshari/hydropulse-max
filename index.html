Microphone Care

Face Down & Avoid Rice Myth URGENT

Never submerge a wet smartphone in rice! Raw rice starch mixes with moisture and forms a hardened paste inside speaker grills. Instead: Power down immediately, hold the speaker grill downward, and let HydroPulse sound waves push water outward.

Seawater, Tea & Soda Immersion CORROSION ALERT

Salt, sugar, and acidic beverages will corrode internal speaker solder within hours! If dropped in the ocean or coffee: Wipe immediately with a damp fresh-water cloth to dissolve salts, dry thoroughly, and run the Extreme Ultrasonic 540Hz Pitch mode.

"Liquid in Port" Warning Override CHARGING PORT

Do not use heat guns or hair dryers—excess heat melts battery seals! Place the phone vertically in front of a cool household fan for 30 minutes, or gently blow clean compressed air at an angle to dislodge droplets from the charging pins.

AirPods Earwax & Muffled Sound EARBUDS

Earwax blocks up to 40% of earbud volume over time. Moisten a clean soft toothbrush with 70% isopropyl alcohol, hold the mesh facing downward so alcohol doesn't drip into the driver, and brush gently outward to dissolve wax buildup.

Microphone Muffled on Calls? MICROPHONE

Never insert sewing needles or metal paperclips into microphone pinholes; this punctures the acoustic waterproof gasket! Press reusable cleaning putty or painter's tape against the hole to lift out pocket lint safely.
