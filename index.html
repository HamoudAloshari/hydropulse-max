Face-Down Gravity Rule URGENT

Always hold your smartphone with the speaker holes facing downward toward the floor. Gravity assists the 165Hz sonic air pressure, allowing expelled droplets to escape immediately without seeping back inside.

Capillary Edge Paper Soak TECHNIQUE

Touch the edge of a dry paper towel against the perimeter of the speaker grill. Do not rub; let capillary action naturally absorb water as HydroPulse pushes it out of the acoustic holes.

Saltwater & Beach Ocean Immersion CRITICAL

Salt crystalizes and corrodes copper voice coils rapidly! Wipe the outer grill immediately with a slightly damp fresh-water cloth to dissolve salts, then run the 540Hz Extreme mode repeatedly.

Tea, Soda & Sugary Drinks STICKY RESIDUE

Sugars harden on speaker cones and muffle volume by over 60%. Moisten a clean cloth with 70% isopropyl alcohol, dab the surface mesh gently, and run the 480Hz Mid-High pitch to break the gluey bond.

"Liquid Detected in Port" Fix CHARGING PORT

Never plug in a charger when this warning appears; it can cause an instant short circuit! Stand the phone upright in front of a cool household fan for 30 minutes to evaporate pin moisture safely.

Silica Gel vs Rice Myth SAFETY TIP

Raw rice leaves starch dust that mixes with moisture to create concrete-like paste inside your ports. Place your device in a sealed container with silica gel packets instead for 10x faster moisture absorption.

AirPods Earwax Dissolution EARBUDS

Earwax blocks up to 40% of earbud volume. Moisten a clean soft toothbrush with 70% isopropyl alcohol, hold the mesh facing downward so alcohol doesn't enter the driver, and brush gently outward.

Earbud Acoustic Air Cavity Flush HEADSET MODE

Connect your Bluetooth earphones and switch to the Headset Clean tab. The dual-frequency sweep pushes lint out from the acoustic air vents while protecting miniature earphone magnets.

Cleaning Putty Gunk Removal MICROPHONE

Press a small ball of electronic cleaning slime or painter's tape against the microphone pinhole and pull it back quickly. Quick presses lift out pocket lint without allowing residue to seep inside.

Never Insert Pins or Needles! DANGER

Inserting metal needles or toothpicks into microphone pinholes punctures the waterproof acoustic mesh, permanently ruining water resistance and destroying the sensitive microphone membrane.
